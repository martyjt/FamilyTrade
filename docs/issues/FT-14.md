## Problem

The app needs a chart-based way to inspect signals, fills and why strategy results differ.

## Dependencies

- https://github.com/martyjt/FamilyTrade/issues/6 — [FT-05] Implement independent candle archives and reproducible dataset revisions
- https://github.com/martyjt/FamilyTrade/issues/12 — [FT-11] Run persistent forward-paper lanes with restart recovery
- https://github.com/martyjt/FamilyTrade/issues/13 — [FT-12] Provide strategy metrics, comparisons and decision evidence
- https://github.com/martyjt/FamilyTrade/issues/14 — [FT-13] Build the private app shell and strategy/run controls

## Scope

- Integrate the existing AG Charts licence using the vendor's supported mechanism; keep licence material out of issue bodies.
- Render bounded candle ranges, signal/fill overlays, equity/drawdown series and linked trade details.
- Provide lane start/pause/stop/status controls using FT-11 and compare saved backtest/paper runs.
- Show actual contract, timezone/session, data mode, gaps, fill ambiguity and current strategy version.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Selecting a trade reveals its chart location, decision inputs and execution assumptions without implying a precise intrabar timestamp.
- [ ] Large ranges use bounded/paginated requests and do not block ongoing ingestion.
- [ ] Two users only see their own data and results; two owned lanes can be compared side by side.
- [ ] Stale feeds, pending runs and reconstructed/delayed/synthetic results are visually distinguishable.

## Validation

Browser tests with representative datasets and screenshots, plus request-bound checks and cross-user access tests.

## Exclusions

Order-book charts, unrestricted technical-analysis scripting, visual workflow graphs and live trading.
