## Problem

Ingestion maintenance and research jobs must survive restarts and avoid competing workers performing the same work.

## Dependencies

- https://github.com/martyjt/FamilyTrade/issues/3 — [FT-02] Freeze the first-slice contracts and golden examples (planning)
- https://github.com/martyjt/FamilyTrade/issues/5 — [FT-04] Implement user identity, broker-account ownership and credential storage

## Scope

- Implement a minimal PostgreSQL-backed job queue and persisted schedules using FT-02 lifecycle contracts.
- Use atomic claiming, bounded concurrency, cancellation, retry limits, heartbeats and recovery of abandoned claims.
- Separate ingestion maintenance from CPU-heavy research execution and apply per-user resource bounds.
- Expose owned-job status and failures through application operations. Schedules must continue without an AI chat session.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Two workers cannot own the same active claim; crashes and expired claims are recovered without silently losing work.
- [ ] Retries rely on operation-specific idempotency; the queue does not claim magical exactly-once execution.
- [ ] Cancellation and time/resource limits lead to explicit terminal or resumable states.
- [ ] Schedule overlap, missed schedules, timezone semantics and restart catch-up are documented and tested.
- [ ] Another user cannot inspect, cancel or modify a job; credentials are not serialized in job payloads.

## Validation

Concurrent-claim, forced-worker-death, retry exhaustion, cancellation, scheduling and tenant-isolation integration tests.

## Exclusions

Celery/Redis/Kafka unless separately justified, Codex automations, distributed orchestration and infinite retry loops.
