## Problem

The archive needs an authorised provider adapter that maintains coverage and reports real connection/data quality.

## Dependencies

- https://github.com/martyjt/FamilyTrade/issues/4 — [FT-03] Prove broker and data access locally and on the Netcup server

## Scope

- Implement the selected provider's per-user historical and ongoing-data adapter behind the agreed contracts.
- Track progress, pacing, finalization/corrections and subscription ownership; persist only eligible data.
- Schedule bounded backfill/gap repair using durable jobs and exchange calendars.
- Expose last received event, last completed candle, feed mode, missing ranges and connection status.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Historical and ongoing feeds join without duplicate or missing boundary bars under the tested provider behaviour.
- [ ] After interruption, the worker resumes from durable progress and repairs eligible gaps without duplicating lane events.
- [ ] A stale or delayed feed is clearly identified; unsupported symbols/subscriptions fail visibly.
- [ ] User sessions and physical archives remain independent while each user's lanes share their owner's data.
- [ ] Expired contracts and provider history limits are surfaced rather than silently replaced by another contract or synthetic data.

## Validation

Adapter fixtures for timeouts/pacing/revisions plus a bounded authorised two-user integration run. Synthetic-only validation cannot close the provider acceptance criteria.

## Exclusions

Other providers, permanent fine-grained archives, live orders and cross-user data sharing.
