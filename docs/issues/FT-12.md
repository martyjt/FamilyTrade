## Problem

Users need an honest account of outcomes and recorded decisions, not just an attractive aggregate profit number.

## Dependencies

- https://github.com/martyjt/FamilyTrade/issues/11 — [FT-10] Persist backtest runs and execute them as owned background jobs

## Scope

- Compute net returns, fees, realised/unrealised P&L, drawdown basis, trade counts, exposure and outcome breakdowns from run records.
- Provide comparison operations with explicit differences in dates, contracts, data quality, costs and strategy versions.
- Expose individual trade/decision evidence, ambiguous fills, delayed/synthetic/reconstructed data labels and incomplete-run status.
- Support explicitly chosen holdout periods and a small bounded cost/parameter sensitivity comparison using ordinary runs; retain attempted variants.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Metrics match independent hand calculations including an open final position, zero trades and losing short trades.
- [ ] Comparisons do not silently mix currencies or equate partial and complete runs.
- [ ] A trade can be traced to the input values and reason recorded when its decision was made.
- [ ] Unseen evaluation dates and all attempted variants remain visible; AI explanations cannot overwrite recorded evidence.

## Validation

Independent metric fixtures, pagination/ownership tests and comparisons with mismatched input assumptions.

## Exclusions

Causal claims, automatic profitability gates, ML training, unrestricted optimisation and UI chart implementation.
