## Scope

- Implement pure evaluation of the reviewed indicator catalogue and bounded entry/exit rule trees, with the agreed risk/session rules.
- Evaluate completed bars only; submit decisions for eligible future fills with exact tick and money arithmetic.
- Model commissions, adverse market/stop slippage, target-limit fills, gap opens and ambiguous stop/target candles.
- Define mark-to-market, cash, realised/unrealised P&L, session exits and open positions/pending signals at run end.
- Use actual contract metadata and reject unsupported contract changes; require explicit lane stop/roll handling instead of silently stitching contracts.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A close-based signal cannot fill inside the candle used to generate it; signal timestamps and fill-bar timestamps remain distinct.
- [ ] Both long and short cases, stop gaps, target gaps, both-hit candles and entry-bar exits follow the documented policy.
- [ ] The same event sequence replayed in batches or incrementally produces identical decisions and fills.
- [ ] Session/DST boundaries, indicator warm-up, daily loss/trade limits and data gaps have deterministic tested behaviour.
- [ ] Every decision records contemporaneous inputs and reasons; end-of-run metrics do not silently assume liquidation.

## Validation

Hand-calculated fixtures for fees, quantity, tick value, short-side exits and equity, plus causality and batch-versus-event replay tests. Synthetic results remain explicitly synthetic.

## Exclusions

Live orders, exchange queue simulation, order-book accuracy, exchange margin emulation and profitability validation.

## Feature catalogue planning clarification

The builder should expose one composable catalogue: price/volume inputs; mathematical
indicators; market structure/regime; and support/resistance features. Keep these as
small pure internal modules where useful, with one typed UI/MCP vocabulary.

Proposed initial examples: OHLCV and relative volume; SMA/EMA/RSI/ATR; explicitly
labelled bar-derived session VWAP; confirmed swing highs/lows and bullish/bearish/
sideways/unknown regimes; prior-session and rolling-window highs/lows as levels.
This is a proposed catalogue, not approval of exact formulas or every listed primitive.
FT-02 must settle definitions, lookbacks, warm-up, session resets, confirmation time,
level-cross/touch tolerance, availability time and complexity bounds before coding.

No lookahead: a pivot requiring later bars becomes usable only on confirmation,
never retrospectively at the pivot bar. Record feature values and rule reasons at
decision time, with versioned definitions. Test long/short, warm-up, equality and
confirmation boundaries. One-minute OHLCV supplies bar volume; exact volume-at-price,
aggressor delta and order-book features require additional source data and are deferred.
